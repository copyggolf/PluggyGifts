## Showroom

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: background for a professional festive 3D mobile/desktop game, landscape 3:2 composition. Build a beautiful Power Buy-inspired appliance kingdom inside a spacious premium electronics showroom. Purple and orange trim, polished softly lit lavender floor, curved ceiling light rails, subtle festive gift ribbon decorations. Along the distant left/right perimeter place clearly recognizable TVs, refrigerators, washers, laptops and small appliances on tidy display plinths. Keep the central 65% completely empty with calm floor and distant walls for gameplay readability, no foreground objects, no characters, no text or logos. Eye-level wide camera with generous floor foreground. Premium cute cinematic 3D materials, soft lighting, subdued background contrast, polished game production art.
