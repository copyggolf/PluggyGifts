# Festive Edition image prompts

Created using the built-in ImageGen tool. The web copies are in assets/v3/showroom.avif and assets/v3/extra.avif. Supplied sign/hammer images and existing original mascot poses are used separately, with no AI repainting of their wording.

## Showroom

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: background for a professional festive 3D mobile/desktop game, landscape 3:2 composition. Build a beautiful Power Buy-inspired appliance kingdom inside a spacious premium electronics showroom. Purple and orange trim, polished softly lit lavender floor, curved ceiling light rails, subtle festive gift ribbon decorations. Along the distant left/right perimeter place clearly recognizable TVs, refrigerators, washers, laptops and small appliances on tidy display plinths. Keep the central 65% completely empty with calm floor and distant walls for gameplay readability, no foreground objects, no characters, no text or logos. Eye-level wide camera with generous floor foreground. Premium cute cinematic 3D materials, soft lighting, subdued background contrast, polished game production art.

## Products and Cable Monster

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent 4 by 4 sprite atlas for a premium cute 3D electronics gift catching game. Exactly 16 distinct single objects, each neatly centered within its own equal square cell, no borders, generous transparent padding, never crossing cell edges. Row1: laptop, tablet, DSLR camera, smartwatch. Row2: robot vacuum, coffee machine, stand mixer, rice cooker. Row3: hair dryer, computer gaming monitor, wireless keyboard, printer. Row4: gaming headset, WiFi router, purple electric toothbrush, dangerous black tangled Cable Monster with angry glowing red eyes and teeth, vivid thin red outline around its silhouette. Polished recognizable realistic 3D product renders, friendly collectible game look, consistent isometric camera and soft studio lighting, no text, no brand logos, no extra decorative objects. Clean truly transparent alpha background including gaps.
